Architecture Highlights

  • Event-Level Dynamic Security: Solves the computational bottleneck of static logging by mapping real-time AI risk assessment $\rho \in \{\text{Low, Medium, High, Critical}\}$ to NIST standardized PQC algorithms.
  • Post-Quantum Resistance: Employs NIST FIPS 204 (ML-DSA-44/65), NIST FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA), and NIST FIPS 203 (ML-KEM) to prevent compromise from future Shor's algorithm quantum adversaries.
  • Off-Chain / On-Chain DLT Scalability: Solves the blockchain bloat crisis of BlockAudit/BlockTrail by keeping high-volume payloads off-chain in store $S$ while maintaining compact cryptographic verification tuples $R = (\text{id}, t, \rho, \text{op}, H(\text{event}), \sigma, \text{ptr})$ on a permissioned Byzantine Fault Tolerant ledger.
  • Fail-Safe Invariant: Fails safe to high-assurance PQC protection whenever classifier confidence drops below threshold or an anomaly is suspected.
